Coffee Lounge & Cocktail Bar

A space that breathes warmth by day and ignites with energy by night — seamlessly transforming between two distinct worlds.

Ground Floor · 7 × 13 m Front Terrace Included Dual-Mode Design
Design Concept

Warmth That Transforms

SOLSTICE draws its identity from the arc of the sun. In the morning, the space blooms with warm timber, soft creams, and the smell of fresh coffee — a neighbourhood café where you linger. After sundown, concealed LED systems activate, the bar illuminates, and the same room becomes a candlelit lounge charged with neon edge.

The neoclassical facade of the Vinhomes shophouse is respected and framed — dark steel windows, arched detailing, and lush landscaping at the front terrace create a destination address before guests even step inside.

Lighting Design

Dual Lighting Systems

Two fully independent lighting circuits share the same fixtures. A single scene-control keypad switches between Day Mode (warm 2700 K) and Night Mode (tunable CCT + RGB LED strips).

☀ Day Mode · Coffee Lounge

Track lighting — dimmable warm-white 2700 K spotlights, GU10 6 W, on ceiling track over all zones. CRI 90+.
Pendant cluster over bar — 5× brushed-brass bowl pendants, Ø 250 mm, staggered heights 1600–1900 mm AFF. 2700 K globe bulb (visible filament style).
Shelf backlighting — warm LED strip 2700 K inside each shelf compartment, diffused through opal diffuser.
Table lamps — freestanding mushroom lamps on banquette tables (amber glass shade, 400 mm high). Reference: Flos Gatto style.
Circular wall fixture — Ø 800 mm halo ring (LED edge-lit, warm white), surface-mounted on Venetian plaster feature wall.
Terrace bollards — 5× low-level bollards at 400 mm, warm white 3000 K, IP65.

☽ Night Mode · Cocktail Lounge

Perimeter LED strip — Emerald Green — RGBW strip along full ceiling perimeter (cove detail), programmed to #00E5A0 neon green. Density 120 LED/m, diffused through opal channel.
Cross-beam neon — Red Accent — two exposed neon-flex tubes (red, #FF3366) running diagonally across ceiling, referencing BÀ BAR aesthetic. 6 mm neon-flex on matte black suspension cable.
Bar pendants dimmed warm — same pendant cluster dims to 20%, casting intimate gold pools over the bar counter.
Under-bar LED strip — green RGBW strip under bar counter overhang, washing the floor with ambient colour.
Pink wash spotlights — 4× RGBW recessed downlights in Zone C switched to deep pink/rose (#FF69B4), creating warm ambient pools.
Linear ceiling battens — 3× black square-profile battens with halo ring detail. Interior LED ring glows white in day, switches to green at night.
